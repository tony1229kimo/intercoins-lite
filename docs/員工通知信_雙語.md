## 中文版

各位同仁 好：

「洲遊幣 Lite」中秋抽獎活動已正式啟動，**活動期間為 2026 年 9 月 1 日至 11 月 30 日**。

這是臺北洲際酒店與高雄洲際酒店合辦的線上遊戲活動。客人**掃描月餅禮盒內附的 QR Code** 即可進入活動頁面，於 LINE 中完成指定任務獲得「洲遊幣」，再投入轉盤抽獎。獎品同時涵蓋兩家飯店。客人若詢問如何參加，**請引導他們掃描禮盒內的 QR Code**。

**這次最需要各位留意的，是本活動有兩種不同的領獎方式，判斷的關鍵在於客人手上有沒有優惠券。**

多數獎項屬於「有優惠券」類：客人中獎當下，優惠券會自動發送到他的 LINE 聊天室，客人自行點選領取後，到店出示兌換碼即可由櫃檯核銷。

另一類則是「由專人聯繫」，包含**臺北洲際酒店的全部獎項**，以及**高雄洲際酒店的兩項住宿大獎**——港灣海景開放式套房住宿一晚（含雙人早餐）與豪華經典房住宿一晚（含雙人早餐）。這類獎項**不會發送優惠券**，畫面會請客人留下姓名、手機、Email 與方便聯繫的時段，後續由該獎項所屬飯店的同仁主動與他聯繫安排。臺北的部分是因為兌換細則尚未公布；高雄的住宿大獎則是因為需要安排入住日期與房型，由飯店主動聯繫較為妥適。

這樣的安排可以清楚區分兩種中獎者：有券的走「櫃檯核銷」流程，沒券的走「專人聯繫」流程，兩邊不會混淆，也不會發生客人拿著需專人安排的獎項到櫃檯要求兌換、現場卻無從處理的情況。

因此有兩點請務必記得。第一，**客人只有兌換碼、LINE 裡沒有優惠券時，請勿核銷**——那是需專人聯繫的獎項，請告知客人將有專人與其聯繫，並回報主管。第二，**客人若反映「中獎了卻沒收到券」，這是正常的**，並非系統異常，請安心向客人說明。

隨信附上《洲遊幣 Lite 員工手冊》，內含客人玩法、櫃檯核銷流程、常見問題的標準回答，以及不可對客人透露的事項。**請於接待客人前務必閱讀完畢**，特別是「常見問題」與「不可以對客人說的事」兩個章節。

如有任何疑問，或遇到手冊未涵蓋的狀況，請聯繫您的單位主管。

感謝各位的配合，祝活動順利！

Tony Chen
Cluster Director of Digital Marketing
InterContinental Kaohsiung & InterContinental Taipei

## English Version

Dear Colleagues,

The "InterCoins Lite" Mid-Autumn campaign is now live, **running from 1 September to 30 November 2026**.

This is a joint online game between InterContinental Taipei and InterContinental Kaohsiung. Guests enter by **scanning the QR code included in the mooncake gift box**, then complete simple tasks within LINE to earn "InterCoins", which they use to spin a prize wheel. The prize pool includes rewards from both hotels. If a guest asks how to take part, **please direct them to the QR code inside the gift box**.

**The most important point this time is that prizes are claimed in one of two ways, and what determines which is simply whether the guest has a coupon.**

Most prizes fall into the "coupon issued" category: at the moment of winning, a coupon is delivered automatically to the guest's LINE chat. The guest claims it themselves and then presents the redemption code at the counter.

The second category is "direct follow-up", covering **all InterContinental Taipei prizes** and **two InterContinental Kaohsiung room prizes** — the Harbour View Studio Suite and the Deluxe Classic Room, each one night with breakfast for two. For these, **no coupon is issued**; the guest is asked to leave their name, mobile number, email address and preferred contact time, and a colleague from the relevant hotel will contact them directly. For Taipei this is because redemption terms have not yet been finalised; for the Kaohsiung room stays it is because dates and room types need to be arranged, which is better handled directly by the hotel.

This cleanly separates the two groups: guests with coupons go through counter redemption, while those without are handled through direct follow-up. There is no overlap, and no situation where a guest presents a prize requiring arrangement at a counter with no way to process it.

Two things to keep in mind. First, **if a guest has only a redemption code and no coupon in LINE, do not redeem it** — that prize requires direct follow-up; please inform the guest that a colleague will be in touch and report it to your supervisor. Second, **if a guest says they won but received no coupon, this is expected behaviour**, not a system error — you can reassure them with confidence.

Please find the *InterCoins Lite Staff Guide* attached. It covers how guests play, the counter redemption procedure, scripted answers to frequently asked questions, and information that must not be disclosed to guests. **Please read it in full before assisting guests**, particularly the "FAQ" and "What not to tell guests" sections.

For any questions, or situations not covered in the guide, please contact your department head.

Thank you for your support — here's to a successful campaign.

Tony Chen
Cluster Director of Digital Marketing
InterContinental Kaohsiung & InterContinental Taipei
