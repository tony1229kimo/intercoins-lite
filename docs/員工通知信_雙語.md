### ⭐ 請特別注意：兩館的獎項，領取方式不同

由於**臺北洲際酒店的兌換細則尚未公布**，兩館採取不同的領獎流程：

| | 高雄洲際酒店的獎項 | 臺北洲際酒店的獎項 |
|---|---|---|
| 客人拿到什麼 | **優惠券自動發送至客人的 LINE 聊天室** | **不會發送優惠券** |
| 如何領取 | 客人於 LINE 中自行點選領取，到店出示**兌換碼**由櫃檯核銷 | 客人於畫面留下**姓名／手機／Email／方便聯繫時段**，由**臺北洲際酒店同仁主動聯繫**安排 |
| 我們要做的 | 依券面規則核銷並交付獎品 | 不需處理，請告知客人將有專人聯繫 |

**這樣的設計可以清楚區分兩館的中獎者：** 高雄的中獎者走「券核銷」流程、臺北的中獎者走「名單聯繫」流程，兩邊不會混淆，也不會有客人拿臺北的獎項到高雄櫃檯要求兌換而無從處理。

> 🚫 **臺北洲際的兌換碼，高雄櫃檯無法核銷。** 獎品名稱前方會標示「臺北洲際酒店」，如遇此情形，請告知客人將有專人與其聯繫，並回報主管。
>
> ℹ️ 客人若反映「抽到臺北的獎卻沒收到券」，**這是正常的**，並非系統異常。

Dear Colleagues,

**The "InterCoins Lite" Mid-Autumn campaign is now live**, running from **1 September to 30 November 2026**.

This is a joint online game between InterContinental Taipei and InterContinental Kaohsiung. Guests complete simple tasks within LINE to earn "InterCoins", which they use to spin a prize wheel. The prize pool includes rewards from **both hotels**.

**Campaign link (may be shared with guests)**
https://liff.line.me/1656533531-r7GGrXqJ

---

### ⭐ Important: the two hotels have different redemption flows

Because **InterContinental Taipei's redemption terms have not yet been finalised**, the two hotels follow different processes:

| | InterContinental Kaohsiung prizes | InterContinental Taipei prizes |
|---|---|---|
| What the guest receives | **A coupon is delivered automatically to their LINE chat** | **No coupon is issued** |
| How it is redeemed | The guest claims it in LINE, then presents the **redemption code** at the counter | The guest submits their **name, mobile, email and preferred contact time**; **the Taipei team will contact them directly** |
| What you need to do | Redeem per the terms shown on the coupon and hand over the prize | No action required — please advise the guest that a colleague will be in touch |

**This design cleanly separates winners from the two properties:** Kaohsiung winners go through counter redemption, while Taipei winners are handled through direct follow-up. There is no overlap, and no situation where a guest presents a Taipei prize at a Kaohsiung counter with no way to process it.

> 🚫 **Taipei redemption codes cannot be processed at Kaohsiung counters.** Prize names are prefixed with "臺北洲際酒店" (InterContinental Taipei). If this occurs, please inform the guest that a colleague will contact them, and report it to your supervisor.
>
> ℹ️ If a guest says they won a Taipei prize but received no coupon, **this is expected behaviour**, not a system error.
