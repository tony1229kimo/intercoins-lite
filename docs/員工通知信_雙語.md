## 中文版

各位同仁 好：

「洲遊幣 Lite」中秋抽獎活動已正式啟動，**活動期間為 2026 年 9 月 1 日至 11 月 30 日**。

這是臺北洲際酒店與高雄洲際酒店合辦的線上遊戲活動。客人**掃描月餅禮盒內附的 QR Code** 即可進入活動頁面，於 LINE 中完成指定任務獲得「洲遊幣」，再投入轉盤抽獎。獎品同時涵蓋兩家飯店。客人若詢問如何參加，**請引導他們掃描禮盒內的 QR Code**。

**這次最需要各位留意的，是兩館的獎項領取方式並不相同。**

由於臺北洲際酒店的兌換細則尚未公布，兩館採取了不同的領獎流程。抽到**高雄洲際酒店**獎項的客人，優惠券會在中獎當下自動發送到他的 LINE 聊天室，客人自行點選領取後，到店出示兌換碼即可由櫃檯核銷。抽到**臺北洲際酒店**獎項的客人則不會收到任何優惠券，畫面會請他留下姓名、手機、Email 與方便聯繫的時段，後續由臺北洲際酒店的同仁主動與他聯繫安排。

這樣的安排可以清楚區分兩館的中獎者：高雄的中獎者走「券核銷」流程，臺北的中獎者走「名單聯繫」流程，兩邊不會混淆，也不會發生客人拿著臺北的獎項到高雄櫃檯要求兌換、現場卻無從處理的情況。

因此有兩點請務必記得。第一，**臺北洲際的兌換碼，高雄櫃檯無法核銷**；獎品名稱前方會標示「臺北洲際酒店」，如遇此情形，請告知客人將有專人與其聯繫，並回報主管。第二，**客人若反映「抽到臺北的獎卻沒收到券」，這是正常的**，並非系統異常，請安心向客人說明。

隨信附上《洲遊幣 Lite 員工手冊》，內含客人玩法、櫃檯核銷流程、常見問題的標準回答，以及不可對客人透露的事項。**請於接待客人前務必閱讀完畢**，特別是「常見問題」與「不可以對客人說的事」兩個章節。

如有任何疑問，或遇到手冊未涵蓋的狀況，請聯繫您的單位主管。

感謝各位的配合，祝活動順利！

Tony Chen
Cluster Director of Digital Marketing
InterContinental Kaohsiung & InterContinental Taipei

## English Version

Dear Colleagues,

The "InterCoins Lite" Mid-Autumn campaign is now live, **running from 1 September to 30 November 2026**.

This is a joint online game between InterContinental Taipei and InterContinental Kaohsiung. Guests enter by **scanning the QR code included in the mooncake gift box**, then complete simple tasks within LINE to earn "InterCoins", which they use to spin a prize wheel. The prize pool includes rewards from both hotels. If a guest asks how to take part, **please direct them to the QR code inside the gift box**.

**The most important point for everyone this time is that the two hotels have different redemption flows.**

Because InterContinental Taipei's redemption terms have not yet been finalised, the two properties follow different processes. When a guest wins an **InterContinental Kaohsiung** prize, a coupon is delivered automatically to their LINE chat at the moment they win; the guest claims it themselves, then presents the redemption code at the counter for processing. When a guest wins an **InterContinental Taipei** prize, no coupon is issued — instead, they are asked to leave their name, mobile number, email address and preferred contact time, and a colleague from InterContinental Taipei will contact them directly to make arrangements.

This design cleanly separates winners from the two properties: Kaohsiung winners go through counter redemption, while Taipei winners are handled through direct follow-up. There is no overlap, and no situation where a guest presents a Taipei prize at a Kaohsiung counter with no way to process it.

Two things to keep in mind. First, **Taipei redemption codes cannot be processed at Kaohsiung counters**; prize names are prefixed with "臺北洲際酒店" (InterContinental Taipei), and if this occurs, please inform the guest that a colleague will be in touch and report it to your supervisor. Second, **if a guest says they won a Taipei prize but received no coupon, this is expected behaviour**, not a system error — you can reassure them with confidence.

Please find the *InterCoins Lite Staff Guide* attached. It covers how guests play, the counter redemption procedure, scripted answers to frequently asked questions, and information that must not be disclosed to guests. **Please read it in full before assisting guests**, particularly the "FAQ" and "What not to tell guests" sections.

For any questions, or situations not covered in the guide, please contact your department head.

Thank you for your support — here's to a successful campaign.

Tony Chen
Cluster Director of Digital Marketing
InterContinental Kaohsiung & InterContinental Taipei
